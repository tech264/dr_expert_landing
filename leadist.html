FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What is Leadist CRM and how does it work?
+
Leadist CRM is an all-in-one sales management platform that helps businesses capture, track, nurture and close leads efficiently. It provides tools for lead management, task scheduling, deal tracking, campaign execution, staff management and detailed analytics — all in one dashboard.
How can Leadist CRM help my business?
+
Leadist CRM eliminates manual tracking, reduces lead leakage, automates follow-up reminders and gives your team a unified view of every prospect. Customers typically see a 2–3× improvement in conversion rates within the first 90 days of using the platform.
How much does Leadist CRM cost? Are there hidden fees?
+
Leadist CRM starts at ₹999/month for the Starter plan. There are no hidden fees — pricing is transparent and scales with the number of staff members. All plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.
Can I try Leadist CRM before subscribing?
+
Yes! Every plan comes with a full 14-day free trial — no credit card needed. You get access to all features of your selected plan during the trial so you can evaluate the platform thoroughly before committing.
Is my data secure on Leadist CRM?
+
Absolutely. Leadist CRM uses bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption, regular automated backups and role-based access controls. Your data is hosted on secure cloud infrastructure and is never shared with third parties.
Does Leadist CRM have a mobile app?
+
Yes, Leadist CRM is available on iOS and Android. Your sales team can manage leads, update statuses, log calls and check tasks from anywhere — giving you full visibility on the go.
